Delete your Evorios account

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Evorios account and associated personal data. App name on Google Play / App Store: Evorios (developer: Evorios LLC).

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Evorios app (or app.evorios.com) and sign in.
  2. Go to Account (profile).
  3. Open Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Active rentals must be finished or cancelled first.

How to request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@evorios.com from the address on your account with the subject Delete my Evorios account. Include the email used to sign in. We will verify ownership and process the request.

What is deleted

  • Profile details (name, email, phone, location labels)
  • Listings and listing photos
  • Messages and in-app preferences tied to your account

Deletion of personal data is completed within 30 days after the request is confirmed.

What may be retained

  • Transaction / payout records that we must keep for legal, tax, or accounting compliance (often in anonymized or limited form)
  • Records needed to resolve disputes, fraud, or chargebacks for the required retention period
  • Stripe may retain payment and identity data under Stripe’s own policies (Evorios does not store card numbers)

Before deletion, pending Stripe Connect payouts are processed where required, and the Connect account is disconnected.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can edit or remove listings and profile fields in the app. For other deletion requests without closing the account, contact support@evorios.com.
