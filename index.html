Questions

Good to know before you start.

What is Evorios?

Evorios is a neighborhood marketplace where neighbors rent and sell household things to each other — tools, sports gear, appliances, baby equipment, and more. Everything is local, so pickup is usually a short walk or drive away.

How much can I earn?

It depends on what you list and how often it's rented, but idle things add up fast — a pressure washer, a kayak, the drill in the corner. Your Evorios dashboard shows what you've earned, your best listings, and a monthly target to grow toward. You start at zero and build from there.

How do I create my first listing?

Create a free account, tap List, and follow the steps — photos, title, category, price, availability. In about 30 seconds your first item is live. Then print or save its QR sticker so handoffs check in and out cleanly.

Do I have to be first? Is an empty neighborhood a problem?

Not at all — being first is the advantage. If few people nearby have started listing yet, the whole neighborhood's demand is yours. Open early, list what you have, and you become the local go-to before anyone else shows up.

How do renting and selling work?

Renting means a neighbor borrows your item for a set period and returns it. Selling means they buy it and keep it. You choose the type when you create a listing, and you can offer the same kind of item either way over time.

How do payments work?

Renters pay in the app when a booking is confirmed. Funds are held for the rental, and you're paid after a successful return. Payments run through Stripe — you don't handle cash for tracked rentals. The full total (rent, any fee, and deposit) is always shown before anyone pays.

What is a QR sticker and why do I need one?

Every rentable item gets a QR code. Scanning it at pickup and again at return confirms the handoff — who had the item, and when. It protects both sides and keeps the rental timeline clear.

How does the deposit work?

Many rentals include a refundable deposit — a hold placed on the renter's card, not a charge. It's released automatically after a clean return. If there's documented damage or loss, the host can claim within the window shown in the app. Evorios isn't an insurer; the deposit is simply a hold.

What if something goes wrong during a rental?

Message the other person first — most things sort out there. If not, open a dispute from the rental and add photos or notes. Evorios is a neutral platform: we give both sides the records, QR timestamps, and messages, and hold the deposit while things are reviewed.

Is my payment and personal information secure?

Yes. Card details are handled by our payment provider, Stripe — Evorios never stores full card numbers. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email, so there's no password to steal. Only ever enter card details in the official app, never in chat.
